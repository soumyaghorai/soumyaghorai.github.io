About

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. (2022–present) in Power Electronics at IIT Kharagpur, under the supervision of Prof. Souvik Chattopadhyay. My research focuses on high-frequency bidirectional AC–DC and AC–AC power conversion systems.

Education

B.E. in Electrical Engineering, Jadavpur University (2017)
M.E. in Energy Science and Technology, Jadavpur University (2019)

Research Interests

“Le vent se lève. Il faut tenter de vivre.” — Hayao Miyazaki

My work explores single-stage isolated AC–DC conversion for high-power EV and PHEV chargers, emphasizing soft switching, wide operating ranges, and high efficiency.
